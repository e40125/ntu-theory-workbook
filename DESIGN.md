# Design direction

A personal theory workbook for daily phone reading and careful paper practice. The opening visual is a small computation trace: input, memory, decision. The persistent chapter rail maps concepts to procedures and proof obligations. The article is the product; navigation stays quiet.

Palette: paper #f5f8fc, white #ffffff, ink #182842, blue #254edb, teal #126c65, amber #8b5500. Light blue is a useful reading surface, with blue reserved for navigation and teal for actual recorded practice. No gradients, stock art, scores pretending to measure readiness, or ornamental metric cards.

Type: Georgia for the long-form explanations and proof text; Trebuchet MS/system sans for headings and controls; system monospace only for actual strings, configurations and transition tables. Body width 69ch, 19px desktop / 18px mobile, generous leading. Equations use locally bundled KaTeX with MathML accessibility.

Layout: desktop chapter rail | article | local lesson anchors when space permits. Mobile folds the rail into a real menu button and keeps a single readable column. Navigation and all practice controls remain available with a keyboard. Print produces the article and reveals solutions intentionally.

[chapter rail] [lesson title + purpose]
               [concrete worked example]
               [formal answer + why]
               [attempt | hint | solution]
               [review evidence + next]

Review against brief: a generic dashboard would bury the learning in cards. Use continuous prose and worked mathematical steps, with compact tables only for genuinely comparable objects. The distinctive element is the computation trace and the proof's claim/reason pairing. Casual tutoring voice; precise formal exam answers. Scope uncertainty belongs in one visible course note. Build standalone, with no account or CDN requirement; save self-reported practice on the device and offer export/import for moving between devices.
